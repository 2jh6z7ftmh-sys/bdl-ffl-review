# League lore

Stories and facts Derek has supplied, by owner. Only what's here gets printed; nothing gets embellished or invented. Add to it as new stories come in.

## Group
- Most owners lived on 6B Witte (B tower, 6th floor) at UW between 1990 and 1992; Brian French earlier. Chad never lived in Witte.
- Derek, Chad, Craig and Adam French: Menasha High, class of 1990. Tom, Ricer, Jim and Rick: Boscobel.
- Almost everyone partied at Derek's Dayton Square apartment (Johnson and Broom), a block from La Bamba's.
- La Bamba's (Broom where it meets State) was the stop after the bars, every time.
- Bullwinkle's, the league's namesake: a short-lived rave-type dance club downstairs under Church Key (University and Lake). None of the owners worked there.
- Housemates: Ricer, Tom, Jim and Rick shared an apartment one year; the next year Abeln, Rusty, Downs, Schwanz and Giff did (Derek thinks 510 West Washington). Mention occasionally.
- The original FFL Review: in 1990 Derek hand-delivered printed copies to 510 West Washington, walking from Witte or Dayton Square. Use once in a while.
- Union Terrace is "the Oasis of this whole thing." Group photo on the giant sunburst chair: `weeks/wk10-statestreet/photos/union_terrace_group.jpg`.
- Old draft rule, before the websites: try to draft a player who's already taken, shotgun a beer. Photo of Tom paying it, with Downs: `tom_downs_shotgun.jpg`.

## Derek
- 6 Time Champ. Worked at the Badger Herald; wrote a column called "Warp Nine."
- Brag when the Ferries win; no mock humility. Self-owns count toward the ribbing balance.

## Ricer
- Huge Red Shed fan; Long Island Iced Teas (potent, mason jars). The group played on the Red Shed's broken pool table; the LITs were the main thing. Group photo at the Red Shed: `red_shed_group.jpg`.
- Original team name: Eureka. Ricer vs Rusty is the league's longest series and famously close (see the 1991–99 Barca–Kodiak log in the project).
- Scored the only zero against Derek: 1993 Week 15, Ferries 32, Barca 0.
- Habit of leaving big points on his bench.

## Rusty
- 21st birthday at State Street Brats: kissed the moose, on his knees on the bar. Kissing the moose was the Brats birthday tradition (per Derek; not found written up anywhere online). Photo: `rusty_moose_brats.jpg`.
- Famous for his time at the KK; the story involves John Hall, the Badgers' kicker, around 1994. Details not yet supplied.
- Carried a mattress back to Chad's, reason unknown. Photo: `rusty_mattress.jpg`.
- Also brought a board over to Chad's (long white board on his shoulder, front lawn). Photo: `weeks/wk10-statestreet/photos/rusty_board.jpg`. Pairs with the mattress: Rusty delivers things to Chad's.
- College argument: Rusty (Sterling Sharpe) vs Abeln (Andre Rison) ended with Rusty doing a Superfly Snuka off the couch onto Abes. No added details.
- Old team: Kodiak Bear; home field "the Spittoon."

## Giff
- Bouncer at Bull Feathers (basement under Stillwaters): "We benefited the hell out of that."
- Banned from Mondays for breaking a gumball machine.
- Badger male cheerleader in the early '90s. Use sparingly.
- Iceman (lightly).

## Chad
- Worked at Church Key and Riley's Wines of the World. Always called the owner "Mrs. Lee." Use Mr./Mrs. Lee in print, no first names.
- The Lees: public court record (2002 and 2009 federal tax cases, bundles of cash in a backpack, Riley's 1995 receipts reported as $6,241). Stick to the record; details and sources in the project's bars10.md.
- Evans Scholar (caddie scholarship); lived in the Evans Scholars house, where the group partied.
- Everton fan. Renames his team constantly; the jokes are fair game except "Tribute Mr French - AKA Mark," which is off limits.

- The Metronome (most consistent scorer). Strong regular seasons, rough playoffs.

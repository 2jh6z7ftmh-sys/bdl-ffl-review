The State Street Bar Tour

Week 10: five games, five stops.

Stop 1

The Red Shed and La Bamba's

Craig2-7 · 1%
at
Ricer7-2 · >99%

First stop: the Red Shed, the little bar with the covered wagon on the roof. Ricer is already there, closer to a shareholder than a regular, and the Long Island Iced Teas are coming in mason jars. Grab a half-size cue; the table sits too close to the walls for anything longer. Pace yourself. The Shed has been pouring these since 1969 and still pours them on State Street.

Save room. The night ends, as always, at La Bamba's, where the burritos are as big as your head and the condiments are negotiable. Craig would know.

Ricer is 7-2 and two games clear in the Right. Craig is 2-7 with 1% playoff odds. The series is closer than either record: Ricer leads 8-7-2 since 2010, and Craig has won two of the last four. Craig's only title came against Ricer, 60-59 in the 2011 final. One point. Ricer may need a Long Island.

Pick Ricer 122, Craig 113 · coin flip

Stop 2

Church Key and Bullwinkle's

Chad5-4 · 79%
at
Schwanz7-2 · >99%

Next, Church Key, where Cheap Trick and George Thorogood once played and Chad is working upstairs tonight for Mrs. Lee, on the books as far as the IRS knows. Head downstairs to Bullwinkle's, a name that may be familiar, and raise your hand if you ever served as Chewie's wingman.

This is a matchup of the last two champions and a rematch of last year's final, which Chad won 172.9 to 123.3. Between Schwanz's title in 2024 and Chad's in 2023 and 2025, these two own the last three. Chad leads the series 15-10-1, but Schwanz took Week 1 by 1.3 and hasn't lost since September: six straight, with the most points in the league. Chad is at 79% and would prefer to settle this one in cash.

Pick Schwanz 128, Chad 121 · coin flip

Back to State Street Brats, where there's a moose behind the bar and a tradition: you kiss it when you turn 21. Rusty is a proud member of that moose's body count. Then over to the KK, where Dog has spent his share of nights. If I go there tonight, will I see four-year Badger kicker John Hall?

Rusty is second in the league in points and 3-6, which takes some doing. He leads the Frenches 13-6 since 2009, but the Frenches have won the last three, including a 165.8 to 71.6 beating in 2024 and Week 1 this year by 0.8. Playoff odds: 31%. The moose is not helping.

Down the stairs to Bull Feathers. There was a time when Giff worked this door, and some of our readers benefited the hell out of that.

Then Mondays, where the drinks hit like a Ron Dayne carry. Would a FOIA request turn up anything on Giff and the gumball machine?

Former roommates Abes and Giff have played 73 times. Giff leads 38-33-2, and the six playoff meetings are split 3-3; Abes won the last one, the 2023 semifinal. Giff is 7-2 with the fewest points of any contender, which is what happens when you know the guy at the door. Abes is 1-8.

We're going to need a cab for this one. Essen Haus. The polka band is warming up and somebody already has a boot going. Toe up, gentlemen. Finish it and the guy who handed it to you buys the next one, so choose your passes carefully.

The oldest rivalry in the league: 73 games since 1990. Tom won the first title that year; Derek leads the series 36-34-3. The last playoff meeting was the 2021 quarterfinal, which Tom won 105 to 52 on his way to his third title. The Ferries have lost two straight and slipped to 68%. Derek is on the road and could use a boot.

Pick Derek 122, Tom 121 · coin flip

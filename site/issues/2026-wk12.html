Home Sweet Home

Theatre of Pain · 1985 · the race for the byes

Three teams are 9-2 with three weeks to play. Two of them get a bye. The third gets the opening slot.

On the Right, Ricer is two games up on the Ferries. If Ricer beats Tom and the Ferries lose to Schwanz, the division is his this week, and he can spend Weeks 13 and 14 posing for the album cover.

On the Left, Schwanz and Giff are both 9-2 and meet in Week 14 at Giff's place. Schwanz has won eight straight on the most points in the league. Giff has won five straight on the seventh-most, which is less a winning streak than a magic trick.

Behind them, Chad is 5-6 and holds third on the Right, with Rusty and Tom a game back at 4-7. The Frenches are 5-6 and third on the Left. Craig, at 2-9, needs to win out, have the Frenches lose out and then win a tiebreaker. Journey wrote the song about not stopping believing, and Journey isn't on this bill.

The Syndicate are the first team out.

      The Final Countdown

      The Final Countdown · 1986 · Schwanz at Giff, Week 14

      If both win their next two, the last week of the regular season is 11-2 against 11-2, for the Left and the bye.

      Two teams tied atop a division have met in the final week once before, in 1991, when Ricer and Rusty were both 8-5. Never with records like these.

      The closest thing was 2018. The Frenches and Schwanz were both 9-2 after Week 11, but they had already played twice, and the Frenches had won both.

      Schwanz leads the series 29-19 and won the first one this year, 147.4 to 124.1 in Week 5. They have met in the last week of the season before: in 1997, Schwanz (9-3-1) beat Giff (8-4-1), 31-24, in Week 14. Giff won the playoff rematch the next week.

      It's the final countdown on the Left, and it ends at Giff's place. Somebody bring the keytar.

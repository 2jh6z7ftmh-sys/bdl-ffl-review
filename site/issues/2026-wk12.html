The race

Run Runaway · Slade, 1984

Three teams are 9-2. Only two get a bye.

Ricer is the runaway in the Right: two games up on the Ferries with three to play. A Barca win over Tom plus a Ferries loss to Schwanz wraps up the division this week.

The Left has no runaway, just two teams running. Giff and Schwanz are both 9-2. Schwanz has won eight straight, Giff five. They meet in Week 14 at Giff's place, which is the kind of scheduling Slade would have written a song about.

Behind them, the chase. Chad is 5-6 and holds third in the Right, with Rusty and Tom a game back at 4-7. The Frenches are 5-6 in the Left, and only Craig, at 2-9, can still catch them: Craig would have to win out and the Frenches lose out. The Syndicate are out.

Odds model (same one as all season, 20,000 sims): Ricer 89% to win the Right; the Ferries above 99% to make the playoffs; Chad 55%, Rusty 30%, Tom 16% for the last Right spot.

Liner note. Noddy Holder and Jim Lea wrote "Run Runaway," and Slade shot the video at Eastnor Castle in Herefordshire. It went to No. 7 in the UK and No. 20 here in 1984, the year after Quiet Riot rode Slade's "Cum On Feel the Noize" up the American charts. A castle, a video, and a top-20 hit. Peak 1984.

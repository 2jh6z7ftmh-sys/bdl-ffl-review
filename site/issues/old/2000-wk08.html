FFL ReviewWeek 8, 2000

Hawaii at Boulder

Hawaii at Boulder
Contrast. The team that won the Championship last year vs. the team that lost the Toilet Bowl. The team that downed Boise last week, propelling them to sole possession of first place and securing a key tiebreaker edge (setting up the second game against Boise the last week of the season) vs. the team that scored 65 points, but still lost by 18 to Bloomington. Boulder has scored 38 pts or more six times this season, but is struggling with a 3-5 record. The Iguanas have won only one of their last six against the Locals.

Iowa City at Boise
Boise suffered an emotional loss last week, getting dumped by Hawaii. Iowa City gained playoff ground with Tennessee’s win over Kodiak. Iowa City’s first game ever was a loss to Boise on 9/3/95. That loss must have left a sour taste in their mouth because they did not lose another game to the Noise until Week One of this year.

Barca City at Bloomington
Pick your stat. The Loungers have dominated this series of late, outscoring Bloomington 83-35 in their last two games. They have averaged 46.25 ppg against the Syndicate since ’95, and they have scored 139 points in the last two weeks. Sorry, Abeln.

Tennessee at Washington Island
Since these two teams met in Week 5, they have proceeded through the season in opposite directions. Tennessee is riding a five game winning streak, while the Ferries are now 1-7 on the year. The Island has a small glimmer in the fact that they have scored 122 points over the last two weeks. Despite their streak, the Tomcats are still two games out of first place.

Kodiak at Chapel Hill
Chapel Hill made a late rally last week, nearly beating Iowa City on the back of Lamar Smith on Monday night. This game features the two teams with the top two picks in the 2000 draft. Too bad their combined record is 5-11. Luck is a foreign word to the Devils this year. They have lost three starters to injury and an appeal so far this season and now they have to go up against Kurt Warner’s replacement, Trent Green, who was promptly picked up by the Bear last week. Maybe they need to relocate. Since ’96, the Devils have averaged 17.6 ppg against Kodiak. Oh, and one other little note. Chapel Hill has never beaten the Bear.

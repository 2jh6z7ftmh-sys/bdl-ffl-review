FFL ReviewWeek 10, 2000

Kodiak at Iowa City

Kodiak (3-6) at Iowa City (5-4)
Kodiak, who leads in this lifetime series 6-4, dropped its sixth straight game last week. The Boozer defense holds their key. Last week was the first time since Sept. 11 that they allowed less than 33 points. The season starts now for both these teams. Looking at the remainder of each team’s schedule, Kodiak plays four of its remaining five games against divisional foes, while Iowa City goes up against five straight divisional teams, including the Bear twice.

Bloomington (4-5) at Chapel Hill (3-6)
In 1997, Chapel Hill swept Bloomington. Unfortunately for them, that was the last victory they had against the Syndicate. Bloomington put up 18 points last week, their lowest of the season, while Chapel Hill has scored 102 over the last two games. If Bloomington is going to hit you, they will hit you hard. They have scored over 80 twice this season (while four teams have not even seen the other side of 60).

Boise (5-4) at Tennessee (5-4)
Both these teams had less than stellar weeks in Week Nine. Boise lost 20-15, while Tennessee fell 22-16. The Noise has lost three of its last four. What does Tom do to put together that stifling defense every year? The Tomcats have not allowed an opponent to score more than 23 points since Sept. 17.

Barca City (8-1) at Boulder (4-5)
The two teams that took our money last year meet for the second time this season (Barca won 36-24 in Week One). The Loungers roll on. They have not lost on the road since week 10 of ’99 and have only scored less than 40 points twice this season (39 and 36 pts). In fact, their offense is so potent, the next highest scoring team is 71 points behind them. Boulder doesn’t mess around with their wins and losses. When they lose, opponents average 59 pts; when they win, opponents average 29.

Wash Island (2-7) at Hawaii (6-3)
Hawaii defeated the Ferries 33-17 in Week 1. Wash Island has only scored more than 22 pts three times this season. Last week was Hawaii’s lowest score of the season (24). The lifetime series has Wash Island leading slightly, 7-6.

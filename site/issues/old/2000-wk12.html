FFL ReviewWeek 12, 2000

Boise at Barca City

Boise (6-5) at Barca City (8-4)
Barca’s loss to Iowa City last week could turn out to be a key one. The Boozers have swept the Loungers and now own the tiebreaker. Barca City’s cushion is only one game. This is a race to watch. Boise’s loss was no less significant. It moved them out of a tie for first place with Hawaii. The Noise have dropped six in a row to Barca City and haven’t bested the Loungers since 1995. The Loungers are on track to top the season scoring record. They are 89 points behind the ’95 Kodiak team with three games to play.

Hawaii (7-4) at Bloomington (5-6)
Hawaii is in control of it’s own destiny and is in position to execute its first ever season sweep of Bloomington. The Local’s 71 points last week was one short of a team record; they scored 72 in ’95. Bloomington’s season hinges on this game and the next one against Boulder (with whom they are tied with for third place).

Chapel Hill (4-7) at Wash Island (3-8)
With last week’s loss to Kodiak, the Ferries are guaranteed their second straight losing season. I want a recount! The Devils are not out of it by any means. They still have key games against Boise and Boulder, they are technically 3 games out of first place and they have a winning division record. Who knows? Anything can happen.

Tennessee (5-6) at Iowa City (7-4)
Iowa took the last contest 58-22. In fact, of the last six games these two teams have played, Tennessee has only won one. Of course that one was the ’99 Wild Card game. Iowa is entertaining hopes of a bye in December. Their season sweep over Barca gives them the tiebreaker over the Loungers, but they still need to make up a game. Tennessee is trying to lose its playoff spot, but isn’t getting much of a challenge from Kodiak (1-8 over last nine) and Wash Island (3-8 this season).

Kodiak (4-7) at Boulder (6-5)
In 1997, Ki-Jana Carter scored 16 for Boulder on the way to a 48-48 tie with Kodiak. Since ’96, the Iguanas are averaging 53 ppg against the Bear. Kodiak is a miserable team on the road. They have won one of their last 14 away from the Spittoon. Boulder could end up the spoiler in the division. Their remaining opponents (Kodiak, Bloomington, Chapel Hill) have a combined 13-20 record this season.

FFL ReviewWeek 2, 2001

Boulder at Kodiak

Boulder (0-1) at Kodiak (0-1)
Rusty just can’t win against the Virgins, whether on cable or on the football field. The Bear got spanked last week 69-32. Unfortunately, they have to play them again on Week 9. Maybe then they can manage a victory (Kodiak has one win in the last 12 games). Boulder, on the other hand, went into last week’s game on an eight game win streak. Unfortunately for the Iguana, that streak ended by one point in a 45-44 squeaker against Tennessee.

Wash Island (0-1) at Sherwood Forest (1-0)
Here’s my favorite stat of the week. With their win last week, Sherwood Forest now has a winning record for the first time since December of 1997. That’s amazing. The top scorer of the week last week was Virgin Ahman Green with 24 points. The Ferries are hungry, but are continuing their early season slumping. Last season they started off 0-6. This year they’re already 0-1 and going against a Virgin team that scored 69 last week. Ugly.

Barca City (1-0) at Iowa City (0-1)
Iowa City was overpowered last week by Hawaii’s Lamar Smith, who scored both on the ground and through the air. The Boozers have a couple of things on their side, however. First, they have had quite a bit of recent luck against Barca. Not only did they hand the Loungers two of their four losses last year, they have taken four of the last five games between these two teams. Couple that with the fact that Iowa City won six of the last seven regular season games last season, and you’ve got a pretty good game. Except when you consider that Barca City scored 56 last week in a well balanced attack that had three players in double digits.

Bloomington (1-0) at Hawaii (1-0)
An early season division game between two teams that combined to score over 130 points last week means Game of the Week. Hawaii looked great last week, but that’s nothing new. They started last year winning 6 of their first 8 games and wound up with a 7-7 record. The question is can they play 14 games. Bloomington leads the lifetime series 10-4-2.

Boise (0-1) at Tennessee (1-0)
Tennessee won in an uncharacteristic fashion last week. They used a strong offense and a porous defense. The final score: 45-44 in a squeaker over Boulder. After this game comes the meat of the Boise schedule. Four straight weeks against each of their division opponents.

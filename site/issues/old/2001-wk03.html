FFL ReviewWeek 3, 2001

Richter Takes Over

After starting the season 0-2 for the third straight year, the Washington Island Ferries announced a complete overhaul of their front office personnel. “Richter’s in charge now. You talk to him, but don’t make him angry!” Wash Island owner Derek Kosiorek said during the press conference announcing the hiring of Richter as the team’s new GM.

“The first thing we do is dump those pathetic pink uniforms. From now on, it’s all black leather,” Richter revealed to the delight of all Wash Island fans.

Week 2

Sherwood (2-0) at Bloomington (1-1)

Announcers: James Blattner, Rodney Hampton

Lifetime: 7-5 (Blo), She has won 3 of last 4

Sherwood Forest takes their 2-0 record to Bloomington, where they have not had an easy time. Even though the Virgins have averaged 47 points over the past 16 regular season games, they have averaged less than 24 ppg lifetime when playing at Bloomington. Sherwood Forest also boasts the best running back corps in the league. Anthony Thomas, Warrick Dunn and Tiki Barber compliment franchise player Ahman Green. Look for head Virgin Todd Martin to entertain trade offers if this keeps up.

Iowa City (0-2) at Hawaii (2-0)

Announcers: Raghu Devaguptapu, Donald Igwebuike

Lifetime: 5-4 (Iow)

Good news for the Locals. They are 2-0 for the first time since 1994, making them a mere six games away from their best season ever. More good news - last year these teams played twice with the home team outscoring the visitors 137-38. Iowa City’s aerial attack has failed to get off the ground this season. Their main weapons Manning, Moss and Bruce have combined for only 40 points so far this young season.

Boise (2-0) at Boulder (1-1)

Announcers: Adam Guenther, Christian Okoye

Lifetime: 11-7 (Bou), Boulder has won 4 of last 5

Boise might not want to win this one. Last year these teams met in Week 3, with the Noise winning 45-34. They then went on to lose 9 of their next 10 games and had their worst season ever. The two teams that missed the playoffs from the Left Division last year are the two top scoring teams so far this year. Boise became the first team to top 100 points in Week 2, winding up at 102. Boulder is sitting in second at 97.

Barca City (1-1) at Tennessee (0-2)

Announcers: Rick O’Donnell, Anthony “The Stain” Miller

Lifetime: 12-10 (Bar)

Even though Barca City has won 5 of the last 6, give the edge to the Tomcats in this one. Why? The lifetime score between these two teams has Tennessee up 666-632. They’ve scored a league low 39 points this season, they haven’t won, they’re angry and they have an evil score vs. Barca. If I was Ricer, I wouldn’t even bring my team to Tennessee this week.

Kodiak (1-1) at Wash Island (0-2)

Announcers: Austin “Rubber” Park, Vice “Pookie” Workman

Lifetime: 13-8 (Kod)

Former Bear running back Ricky Williams guaranteed a victory this week, taking a queue from his new GM. “See you at the party, Rusty,” he said when asked about the matchup. Kodiak dominated the Ferries in the early ‘90s, winning nine of the first ten games between these two teams. The eleventh game marked the end, however, when the Ferries buried the Bear 42-3 in Week 13 of 1996.

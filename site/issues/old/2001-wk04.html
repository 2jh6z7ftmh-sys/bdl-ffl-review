FFL ReviewWeek 4, 2001

Kodiak at Barca City

Boise (2-1) at Sherwood Forest (2-1)
Boise has been playing on fumes lately. In the past three weeks, they have been decimated by injuries. They lost their franchise player (Fred Taylor), their top pick (Terrell Davis) and their third round pick (Ed McCaffery), but they still mounted one hell of a comeback last week, scoring 29 points late (Sunday night) to come from behind and step on Boulder. The friendly confines of Sherwood have been good to the Virgins. In two home games, they scored 114 points. On the road? 38. This is good for the Virgins becuase their next four games are at home, but bad because five of their last seven are on the road.

Kodiak (1-2) at Barca City (2-0-1)
Last year, I wrote, "over one-third of all games played between these two teams have been decided by two points or less. This is the 21st game between the Bear and the Loungers. Kodiak has dropped nine straight to Barca dating back to 1995." So what happens after I write this? They tied in Week 13. Since Barca won the other game, going into the 23rd game in this rivalry, Kodiak still has not won since 1995. Unfortunately for the Bear, it looks like the trend will continue. Barca is the top scoring team in the league, averaging 55 ppg and Kodiak is the worst defense in the league, allowing the same amount that Barca scores. Kodiak is hoping that its recent trade for Isaac Bruce will pick up the offense, but it won't matter if they continue to use the "sieve" defensive formation.

Bloomington (3-0) at Iowa City (0-2-1)
If you go by the numbers, this game is already decided. The only 3-0 team against the only team without a win; the Syndicate have not lost to the Boozer since '98; Iowa City has the second worst defense in the league, allowing over 53 ppg; and last year, Bloomington racked up 76 points in one game against IC. Then again, if Randy Moss gets off his ass, the Boozer has a chance.

Wash Island (1-2) at Boulder (1-2)
How bad is the Ferry offense? Last week they scored 24 points. This was their highest score of the season. They have scored 101 points less than Barca. That's over 33 points per game. It was time for a change, so management decided to ship Isaac Bruce to Kodiak in exchange for Mike Anderson and Antonio Freeman. As far as Boulder is concerned, even though they have the top scoring player this season in Peyton Manning, he is not enough to save a decimated backfield. Their first round pick, Michael Bennet hasn't panned out and Jamal Anderson just went down for the season. To boot, Boulder has only beaten the Ferries once since 1993, and that was in '97. Starting slow is not strange for Boulder, however. Last year they went 3-5 before running the table.

Hawaii (1-2) at Tennessee (1-2)
Tennessee finally allowed less than 40 points last week, but their offense couldn't help them out. They lost by a point to the lowly Ferries. The Tomcats have gotten consistent scoring out of QB Brett Favre, but need RB Eddie George to start picking up some slack if they want to be successful. Hawaii is picking up where it left off last season, with solid offense, but problems finishing off the opposition when they get ahead. Going back to last season, they have lost seven of their last nine games. This is extra suprising considering they are averaging 43 points over three games so far this season. To start the season last year, the Locals were 6-2, so maybe they'll finish this season like they started the last.

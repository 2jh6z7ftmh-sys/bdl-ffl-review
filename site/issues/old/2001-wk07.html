FFL ReviewWeek 7, 2001

Iowa City at Barca City

What's this? Six games and only 3 teams are over .500? Doesn't matter. The meat of the schedule is coming up. Eight of the ten teams have at least five of their eight divisional games coming up between now and the end of the season.

Iowa City (2-3-1) at Barca City (4-1-1)
These two teams last met back in Week 2, which ended up in a 40-40 tie. Barca City is playing well, as usual. In fact, the Loungers haven't had a losing record since Week 3 of 1997. They're not scoring the most, but they get it when they need it. This week they face an Iowa City team that gets its meat back. The last two games that Jeff Garcia and Terrell Owens played, they combined for 85 points. Last week (without the dynamic duo), the Boozer could only muster 26. By the way, Iowa City has the worst defense in the league, allowing 49.6 ppg.

Tennessee (3-3) at Bloomington (5-1)
The second top scorer in the league, Tennessee's Brett Favre, is taking the week off just when the Tomcats need him most. Bloomington couldn't be in a better position. They have the best offense in the league, with 54.5 ppg and the best defense in the league, allowing just 32.5 ppg (in case you're wondering, the average score per game for all teams is 41.7). Curtis Martin finally proved he is human last week. It was his first game this season scoring less than double digits. More good news for the Syndicate - four of their five wins were division games. Both teams are looking for their third win in a row.

Boise (2-4) at Kodiak (2-4)
On the other hand, Boise and Kodiak are looking to stop the bleeding. Boise is on a three game losing streak and Kodiak has two straight losses. I have to give the advantage to Kodiak, however. The Noise has managed to collect only 28 points over the last two games ... combined. On the other hand, the Bear has not defeated the Noise since Week 2 of 1998.

Boulder (2-4) at Sherwood Forest (3-3)
After five solid weeks, the Virgins finally crashed, managing only 17 last week. What started off as a marriage made in heaven has fallen flat. Kurt Warner and Ahman Green have combined for only 20 points over the last two weeks. Both these teams are trying to stop a two game losing streak. Boulder has won four straight against Sherwood (then Chapel Hill).

Hawaii (4-2) at Wash Island (2-4)
The Locals are having a great open to their season, as usual (the back half is where they typically fall). Hawaii takes a solid three game win streak into Wash Island, averaging 54 over the span. Daunte Culpepper has emerged as the top scorer in the league with 84 points. In fact, Hawaii boasts four of the top 15 scorers (that's almost one-third, for those of you who went to Iowa). The only redemption for Wash Island's 2-4 record is that both wins and only one loss is in the division.

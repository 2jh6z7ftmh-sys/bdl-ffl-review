FFL ReviewWeek 13, 2001

Hawaii at Bloomington

Hawaii (6-6) at Bloomington (7-5)
This is an interesting week for the Left Division. A lot of questions could be answered. Three teams are gunning for the Division win and only Boise has been eliminated from the playoffs. Hawaii and Boulder are the teams that need to win, but unfortunately (or fortunately depending on the way you look at it), Hawaii plays both Bloomington and the Virgins. Hawaii has not been playing strong lately - they have failed to pass 30 in three of their last four games. After this game, the Syndicate don't exactly have a cake walk with their last game. It's against Barca City.

Sherwood Forest (8-4) at Boulder (6-6)
With the big win over Tennessee this week, Sherwood Forest became the second team this season to clinch a playoff spot.

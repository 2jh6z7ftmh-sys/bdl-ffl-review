HUGE F’N WEEKEND!

FFL history will be made Sunday.

For only the second time in the history of the Bullwinkles Dance League, two teams will meet in back to back Championship games. Next Sunday, Kodiak and Boise will face off for the FFL title for the second time in two years.

What’s at stake? How about history? Both teams were founded in 1991 and both were in the championship game last year, but that’s pretty much where the similarities end.

Boise owner Woody Dean is used to this feeling. This will be the fourth straight finals for the Noise and, if they win, they will be the first team to win three consecutive championship games. Two teams have repeated, but no team has been able to achieve the coveted three-peat. In 1999-2000, Boulder took two championships on the back of Emmitt Smith. Steve Young and Ricky Watters lead Bloomington to the promised land in 1993-94. Both teams failed to make the finals the third year.

On the other hand, Kodiak owner Rusty Halverson is one of two owners who has never tasted the payoff of a championship season. This is Kodiak’s fourth trip to the final game, but will history finally shine on the Bear?

Boise’s biggest fan this week may be Barca City owner Sean Rice. Ricer does not want to be the last owner to never have a championship ring. 2005 was only the second year in the past nine that the Loungers missed the playoffs, but their bids at a league championship have been just as futile as Kodiak. Will Barca be the last team standing (or should I say … sitting)?

No Monday night game means a TON of questions will be answered Sunday. This is turning out to be a landmark weekend in the history of Bullwinkles.

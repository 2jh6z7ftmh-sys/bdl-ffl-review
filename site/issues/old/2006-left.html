FFL Review2006 Draft Preview

On the Left

With the draft approaching, there is the first in a series of two articles previewing the draft for all ten FFL teams.

Bloomington Syndicate
Drafting: 8th
Likely keepers: Shawn Alexander, Willis McGahee
Dark horse keeper: Carson Palmer
Prospect: Bloomington continues to be the most consistent team in the league in many aspects. Each year, the Syndicate has exactly the right amount of keepers, no more no less. Of course, it’s not that hard when you have longtime superstar Shawn Alexander. The addition of Willis McGahee puts Bloomington in a position that most teams strive for – two RB keepers. This will allow them to focus on a star QB or WR while everyone else is focusing on filling in the crucial second RB.

Boise Noise
Drafting: 9th
Likely keepers: Ladanian Tomlinson; Tiki Barber
Dark horse keeper: Torry Holt
Prospect: The Noise lost in the FFL Championship, but they are still riding a crest that has brought them into the final game four years in a row. Interestingly, the Noise don’t have the two really strong keepers that you would expect from a powerhouse team. Tomlinson is a no-brainer and Barber is good, but not a lock. The real question is whether Boise owner Woody Dean will be true to his perennial WR Torry Holt, who has been catching balls for the Noise since 2002.

Boulder Giant Iguanas
Drafting: 4th
Likely keepers: Larry Johnson, Chad Johnson
Dark horse keeper: Stephen Jackson
Prospect: The Iguana was one of the few teams in FFL history to successfully make the transition between a starting RB to a backup. Last year Boulder picked successfully bet on Larry Johnson to take TDs away from Priest Holmes. The gamble paid off, resulting in Johnson arguably being the most coveted player in the league. The team’s second keeper is a bit fuzzier, but Johnson should prevail. Stephen Jackson was one of Boulder’s keepers last year, acquired in a trade with Iowa City. This, along with the fact that he is a starting RB, may make it tougher for Boulder owner Brian Gifford to let go of Jackson. Watch for a possible trade to allow Gifford to move up in the middle rounds.

Iowa City Boozers
Drafting: 6th
Likely keepers: Clinton Portis, Peyton Manning
Dark horse keeper: Kevin Jones
Prospect: Facing a keeper QB is never an easy pill to swallow in the Bullwinkles league. The superiority of the RB and WR positions makes it hard to justify a guy who only gets 3 pts per TD. There have been cases, however, when the QB position is very lucrative. Steve Young, Brett Favre, Mike Vick and Manning have made teams very happy in past years. If the Boozer keeps Manning, they will be the only team to keep a QB this year. Portis isn’t going anywhere but Iowa City this year. His bond with head Boozer Sean Svoboda is well documented. He has been a vial Boozer since being acquired in a trade with Boise during the 2002 season.

Sherwoood Forest Virgins
Drafting: 1st
Likely keepers: Fred Taylor, Hines Ward
Dark horse keeper: Aaron Brooks
Prospects: The Virgins have a good chance to be competitive this year, but nothing is going to come easy for them. They have some work to do. Let’s be honest, these aren’t exactly quality keepers. In fact, it’s doubtful that anyone on the team would be kept if they were somewhere else. On the other hand, they have the first pick in the draft and, through trades, wound up with an extra second round pick to replace their third round one. A trade can get a quality keeper to compliment Taylor and the first pick can get the next best player in the league. If they do nothing going into the draft, however, it’s going to be another tough year.

FFL Review2006 Draft Preview

On the Right

With the draft approaching, here is the second in a series of two articles previewing the draft for all ten FFL teams. If you missed the preview of the Left Division, it’s available here.

Barca City Loungers
Drafting: 3rd
Likely keepers: Rudi Johnson, Edgerrin James
Dark horse keeper: Cedric Benson
Prospect: Barca City is one of a few teams with multiple “keepable” players. Cedric Benson’s stock is rising fast, which may lead to a tough decision for Lounger owner Sean Rice. Does he allow Barca City fan favorite Edgerrin James to suit up for another FFL team? Also on the Barca roster is Javon Walker, who may leap into the upper echelon of receivers this year.

Gary Redeyes
Drafting: 5th
Likely keepers: Domanick Davis; Plaxico Burress
Dark horse keeper: Jeremy Shockey
Prospect: A young man named Reggie Bush really messed with the long term plans of the Gary Redeyes. It was only two seasons ago when the Redeyes looked to give a long term franchise tag to Deuce McCallister. The drafting of Bush essentially made McCallister useless. Domanick Davis is now the only player on the team worth keeping. Watch for Gary owner Chad Brantmeier to make at least one pre-draft trade.

Kodiak Bear
Drafting: 10th
Likely keepers: Terrell Owens, Ronnie Brown
Dark horse keeper: Daunte Culpepper
Prospect: The reining champs look to have a typically busy offseason. Kodiak has already moved Cadillac Williams and team owner Rusty Halverson would like nothing more than to add some more picks. Sources inside the Bear organization say that Halverson is strongly considering slapping the franchise tag on Culpepper. While most would say this is crazy, it would fit right in with the organization’s long standing motto “logic is for losers.”

Tennessee Tomcats
Drafting: 7th
Likely keepers: Steve Smith, Lamont Jordan
Dark horse keeper: Jamal Lewis
Prospect: It’s not saying much that Tennessee won the division last year. They were the only team with a winning record. The keepers for this year are no-brainers. Smith and Jordan are two of the top ranked fantasy players. Jamal Lewis is coming off of his worst year yet and his only appeal as a player is that he’s an ex-con.

Wash Island Ferries
Drafting: 2nd
Likely keepers: Randy Moss, Cadillac Williams
Dark horse keeper: Willie Parker
Prospects: Moss and Williams stand head and shoulders above the rest of the roster, so that’s the way they will likely go. This is, of course, unless the Ferries move Moss, who has fallen out of favor with Island fans. His antics and lack of productivity of late combine to make many question his standing as a Ferry.

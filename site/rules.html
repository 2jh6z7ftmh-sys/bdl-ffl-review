League constitution · 2026

The Rulebook

For the next time somebody asks in August.

01

The League

  • 10 teams, two fixed divisions. Right: Derek, Ricer, Rusty, Tom, Chad. Left: Abeln, Craig, Giff, Frenches, Schwanz.
  • Regular season: 14 weeks. Ties count.
  • Playoffs: Weeks 15 to 17. Top three in each division. Division winners get a bye; 2nd plays 3rd. Playoff ties are broken by backup K points, then backup QB, then all other bench points.
  • Standings tiebreakers: record, head-to-head, division record, points for, fewest points against.
  • 3rd- and 5th-place games are not a thing.
02

Rosters

  • Lineup: QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, FLEX (RB/WR/TE), K, DST.
  • Bench up to 7, IR up to 2, 18 players max.
  • IR is only for players tagged Questionable, Doubtful or Out. CBS will let you stash anyone. The constitution won't.
  • Lineup lock: 5 minutes before each player's own game.
03

Draft and Keepers

  • Auction draft, $200 per team (since 2021; a snake draft before that).
  • One keeper. Cost is last year's salary plus $5. The cost basis travels with the player in a trade.
  • CBS has shown wrong keeper prices before. Check the math by hand.
04

Moves

  • Waivers / FAB: $200 season budget; $0 bids allowed. Waivers run every night except Monday.
  • A winning bid becomes the player's salary, which sets next year's keeper cost.
  • Trades need commissioner approval. The real deadline is the week the first team is eliminated from playoff contention, whatever CBS says.
05

Scoring

PassingTD 4 (+2 for 40+ yards) · INT −2 · 0.1 per 2.5 yards
Rushing / receivingTD 6 · 0.1 per yard · 1 per reception (full PPR) · 40+ yard bonuses
Fumble lost−2
KickingFG 3 (+1 for 40–49, +2 for 50+) · missed FG −2 · XP 1 · missed XP −2
DSTSack, INT, fumble recovery 1 each · TD 6 · safety 2 · points allowed from +10 (shutout) to −4 (35+)
06

HISTORY

How the Rules Changed

1990–2005Touchdown-only scoring: 3 for a passing TD, 6 for rushing and receiving, plus yardage bonuses and kickers. Team scores ran in the 20s to 40s. Defenses scored only for TDs and shutouts.
1990s–2000sPick-order draft, in person. Draft a player who's already taken and you shotgun a beer.
2002Up to two "franchise players" per team. Proposals to expand to 12 teams in 2003 went nowhere.
2006Vote passed DST points for points allowed, a cash prize for most points, and flexible RB/WR lineups. Shortening the season, a fee increase and 12 teams all failed.
2010Keeper cost based on draft round.
2015–202013-week regular season; playoffs in Weeks 14–16.
2021Auction draft ($200), one keeper at salary plus $5.
